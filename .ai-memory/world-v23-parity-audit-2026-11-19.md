The owner identifies the 89.7-second v23 showroom walkthrough as the best World Builder demonstration so far. Its defining experience follows the same room and objects through capture, reconstruction, object review, drawings and a robot fit decision. The newer feature showcase intentionally uses several examples.

The named original 1080p phone recording was located locally. The historic reconstruction run had been moved off the Mac. Four archived file checksums match its original transfer manifest. The run records 48 views and 5,548,392 points. Its 31 proposed instances include 7 carts, 20 tire stacks and 4 tires. The drawing set reports 16 object envelopes and 23 walls across 12 review sheets. The ground routes record 18.69 m and 13.63 m after removing a cart. These are separate artifact stages. Field dimensions remain unverified and the film’s drone trajectory was authored.

The primary parity gaps are an accessible preloaded showroom, linked video/detections/points/object map with shared time and selection, and carrying the selected object’s reviewed footprint into same-room replanning and fleet comparison. Existing PDF generation, camera views, ground routing, baseline comparison and robot-size checks can be reused. The v23 film is composed from artifacts and simulation renders; it does not establish public one-click processing or physical robot execution. Private source and site images stay outside the repository.
