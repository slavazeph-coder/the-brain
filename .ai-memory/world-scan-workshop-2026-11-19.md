# Arrange a room in World Builder

The short link e_EiGqpm is Eros Carrasco's SplatLab at Adobe MAX. The author describes object segmentation, cross-scan reuse, completion and relighting. His public browser example uses pre-separated office assets. The underlying inference engine was not identified as a licensed public SDK. BrainSNN uses independently written processing and its own practice rooms. [Original post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/eros-carrasco_asusproart-adobemax-gaussiansplatting-ugcPost-7528984969289863168-CoR6)

The editor opens inside Make it yours on the scanedit tab. It accepts PLY/SPLAT and preserves the GLB inspector. Tap a visible part or use the keyboard to suggest connected, similarly coloured samples. Refine and review, move/hide/copy, keep on a temporary shelf or save a .brainobject file, open a second scan of the same kind and place it. The authored PLY and receipt bind both sources and preserve the object vertex IDs. Units are local and placement is manual. The Gaussian preview lamp adds a spatial colour effect. The labelled video captures the unlit artifact.
