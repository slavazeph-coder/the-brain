# BrainSNN Marketer-First UX Upgrade

## Primary audience

Marketing, brand, communications and content teams who need to understand how a draft may affect attention, trust and action before publication.

## Product promise

**See what your content does to attention, trust and action before you publish.**

BrainSNN should lead with the decision a marketer needs to make. The underlying 103-layer engine, GaugeGap experiments, Crumb work and technical diagnostics remain intact, but appear as deeper paths rather than competing first impressions.

## Information architecture

- `/` — marketer-first BrainSNN homepage with an immediate scan entry point
- `/app` — guided content workflow
- `/foundry` — existing GaugeGap Foundry experience
- `/reconstruct` — existing claim reconstruction workflow
- Research remains accessible from the app and homepage

## First-session path

1. Choose a content type or paste a draft.
2. Run one analysis.
3. See a five-part summary: overall impact, attention, trust, emotional pressure and risk.
4. Improve the draft.
5. Compare original and rewrite.
6. Save, approve or export.

## Homepage hierarchy

1. Clear marketer-facing headline
2. Large paste box
3. Example chips: Ad, Email, Social post, Headline
4. Primary CTA: Analyze content
5. Secondary CTA: Explore the science
6. Brief three-step explanation
7. Use cases and proof
8. Separate paths to GaugeGap, Research and Developers

## Navigation

### Primary

- New Analysis
- Improve
- Compare
- Library
- Labs

### Grouped destinations

- Library: History and Approvals
- Labs: Research, GaugeGap and Reconstruct
- Pricing: account/upgrade area rather than a primary workflow step

## Preservation rules

- Do not alter the analysis engine or scoring behavior during the navigation upgrade.
- Preserve local history and approval storage formats.
- Preserve existing route aliases and deep links where possible.
- Move GaugeGap; do not delete or flatten it.
- Keep advanced outputs available through progressive disclosure.
- Make all work on a dedicated branch and merge only after build, test and responsive checks pass.

## Success criteria

A first-time marketer should be able to answer these questions within ten seconds:

- What does BrainSNN do?
- What should I paste?
- What will I receive?
- What should I click first?

A first-time user should complete a scan without needing to understand SNNs, solitons, TRIBE, Crumb or GaugeGap.
