# Video pilot scope and evidence

The deployed BrainSNN homepage and engine describe bounded content analysis
and simulation. The separate `/coherence` page is a manual service evaluation,
not a validated restoration product or a deployed learned video model.

The page previously presented synthetic drift/detail/motion percentages and
called a locally prepared form “Received.” The form had no intake POST or
persistence. The corrected flow prepares an owner email draft containing the
clip link, reply email, problem and proposed US$99 acceptance terms. The user
reviews and sends it in their mail app. Preparing the draft is not a submitted
lead, customer acceptance or payment.

`/coherence-evidence.html` separates post-process fixtures from learned-memory
research. It names full-attention, EMA, frozen-frame and explicit-reference
baselines; identifies hardware, sample/seed scope and trained status; and
records failed native-resolution gates. Exact hardware or seed metadata that
was not saved remains unknown.

The later owned 1280×720 house clip passes detail but fails motion (0.516330
against a 0.90 floor). Its unmodified receipt is served at
`/research/video/native-720-house-20261013.json`. It is not a stranger clip or
customer result. Failed outputs are ineligible for paid rescue fulfillment.

The 417× video-token claim, untrained-stream coherence, global synthetic
quality percentages and superiority over deployed linear models are retired.
The learned engine still requires three initialization seeds on the same
256-frame dependency, relevant linear baselines, leakage/state-intervention
checks and held-out decoded-video review.

The older `ui/brainsnn-site` copy also now distinguishes Crumb LLM research
from the current deterministic content detector. The existing homepage,
navigation, colors, layout, motion and comparison media are preserved.

## Verification and release

The mailto tests preserve user-supplied URL query characters and keep them
inside the encoded email body. Browser tests exercise the full preparation
flow on desktop/mobile, assert the honest status and actual mailto fields,
and confirm that the evidence page/receipt are real static resources.
Browser verification must never click the external mailto link or send mail.

This correction requires a reviewed merge and the existing Railway deployment
before it changes the live site. Verify deployed semantic markers and receipt
contents; a local build or draft PR is not a deployment receipt.
