    What a walk gives you, and what it doesn't
    Map position
    From the phone's GPS. Each walk states the radius that 95% of its fixes were within. Outdoors that is usually a few metres; indoors there is often no fix at all, and the walk says so.
    Features
    Each tap drops a feature with the type you chose, the time, a photo, and the nearest GPS fix. A feature without a fix gets no invented position.
    Shape accuracy
    Not measured. A phone camera walk does not measure shapes or distances. Build the 3D world from the walk's video, then check one known length before trusting dimensions.
    Exports
    A bundle with GeoJSON and GPX (QGIS, ArcGIS, Google Earth), a spreadsheet of features, the photos and the video.
    Install
    On iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android: the browser menu, then Install app.
