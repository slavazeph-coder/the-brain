What to bring for the first gym

  • A map or walkthrough of one empty, level floor zone, plus a dimensioned sketch if no supported map export is available.
  • Two measured distances to check scale. For RoomPlan, the capture app must export CapturedRoom JSON with a floor polygon; a USDZ-only export is insufficient.
  • Machine and rack footprints, free weights, mats, mirrors, glass, ramps and stairs. Exclude floor that should not be cleaned by this robot.
  • The cleaner's measured width, depth, cleaning width and working speed, including attachments.
  • Setup/finish time, expected assistance, manual cleaning rate and refill/service needs.
  • Your labour/hour, robot/hour and consumables/m² rates. The cost stays blank until you enter your rates.

Read the result correctly

OutputHow it is estimated
Robot runThe planned route at your entered speed, plus modelled turn delays and configured service stops.
Human helpSetup, finishing, entered assistance events, service labour and optional manual follow-up for uncovered open floor.
Sequential completionRobot work plus human work under a serial assumption, counting shared servicing once. It does not predict parallel staff scheduling.
Cost per runHuman hours × labour rate + robot run hours × robot rate + worked area × consumables rate.
Uncovered floorGrid-estimated open floor outside the planned cleaning swath. Inspect these areas before deciding who cleans them.

The route is a bounded geometric preview. It is not an optimized cleaning controller or a measured robot trial. Assistance is modelled from your inputs; it is not a learned failure prediction. The selected GR00T model does not execute in this estimate.
