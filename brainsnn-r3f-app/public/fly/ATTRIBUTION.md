# Fly feeding circuit — data attribution

`feeding-circuit.bin.gz` is a 2,621-neuron, 195,759-connection slice of the
FlyWire v783 *Drosophila melanogaster* connectome, with neuron positions from
FlyWire Codex and the whole-brain outline mesh from FlyWire's public neuropil
meshes. It is built by `scripts/fly/build-fly-circuit.py`.

- Connectome: FlyWire Consortium — Dorkenwald et al., "Neuronal wiring diagram of
  an adult brain", *Nature* 634 (2024); Schlegel et al., "Whole-brain annotation
  and multi-connectome cell typing of Drosophila", *Nature* 634 (2024).
- Model and packaged connectivity: Shiu et al., "A Drosophila computational brain
  model reveals sensorimotor processing", *Nature* 634 (2024).
  https://github.com/philshiu/Drosophila_brain_model (MIT licence).
