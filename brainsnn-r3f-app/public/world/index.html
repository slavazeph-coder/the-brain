Process on BrainSNN

BrainSNN's own server reconstructs your walkthrough on CPU. Your files are uploaded there, used only for your job and deleted within 24 hours.

Or connect a worker of your own:

Private beta. Use an operator-issued credential. Your token stays in page memory. Public signup is not available.

Identify objects

Download an object detector and runtime from Hugging Face and jsDelivr (about 55 MB on first use). It runs on this device. Your photo is not uploaded.

DETR ResNet-50 (Apache-2.0) suggests labels from 80 common categories such as chairs, tables, beds, couches, TVs and people. It does not know doors, stairs, boxes or cables, and a missed object is not evidence of free space.

Every suggestion waits for your review. Labels never change scale or collision geometry.

Create a 3D preview

Download a small depth model and runtime from Hugging Face and jsDelivr (about 50 MB on first use). The model runs on this device. Your photo is not uploaded.

Depth Anything V2 Small estimates visible surface depth. This preview has no measured scale and cannot reveal hidden surfaces. It is not a robot collision map.

Downloads may be cached by your browser. You can cancel processing at any time.
