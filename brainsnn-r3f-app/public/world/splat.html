The viewer and parser run on your device; no third-party renderer is loaded. Up to 100,000 points are displayed, with uniform sampling for larger files. Requires WebGL2. Gaussian assets use base colour only; view-dependent shading is not rendered. GLB must be self-contained, without Draco, Meshopt or KTX compression. Export SPZ / KSPLAT assets as uncompressed PLY or SPLAT first.

A point cloud is not a trained Gaussian splat. Neither establishes metric scale, collision geometry or robot safety. Unobserved surfaces remain unknown.
