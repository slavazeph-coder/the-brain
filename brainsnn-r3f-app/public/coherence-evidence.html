Compute receipts are not coherence receipts

The historical 417× figure timed one unwarmed CPU mixing operation; it did not measure token savings. A warmed receipt gives 0.968ms FFT versus 857.605ms full scaled dot-product attention at N=16,384 (886×): x86_64 CPU, four threads, fp32, one 64-channel head, two warmups and median of five repetitions. No weights were trained for this timing, and its CPU model/RNG seed are unrecorded. Raw operation receipt.

That comparison does not establish GPU speed, end-to-end generation cost or superiority over deployed windowed, state-space or factorized spatial/temporal attention. The VAE sets the token count for both arms.

The 7,200-frame constant-state stream used untrained weights and froze at frame zero. It measures streaming cost. A seed-0 CPU clean-write probe learns a 128-frame blank-background recall task; this is not textured-video memory or three-seed 256-frame learned recall. Training protocol and limits.

What would support a stronger result

Train the same 256-frame dependency on at least three initialization seeds. Compare explicit reference/latch memory, frozen frames, bounded-window attention, generic SSM and factorized attention under declared resource budgets. Verify no future/oracle inputs, actual state-erasure and donor dependence, no-event false presence and held-out source separation.

A frozen pretrained VAE is a codec, not a generative prior. Validate encoder causality, decode held-out predictions, report the first collapse, and obtain blind native-resolution review before claiming better generated video.

A customer result additionally needs an authorized independent clip, a useful accepted output and a recorded payment. Drafts, owned footage, analytics and AI-authored reviews do not establish demand or replace independent human research review.
