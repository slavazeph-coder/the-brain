01 / BRAND LAUNCH

Make an entrance.

A product reveal with a distinctive physical centrepiece and a clear next step for your audience.

Example: launch-day showcase +

A software brand sponsors the hood for an agreed launch activation. A dedicated landing page connects the reveal content to a product trial. Track visits and opted-in inquiries, not assumed impressions.

02 / TECHNOLOGY DEMOAI + ROBOTICS
FROM ATTENTION TO ACTION

Show what you do.

Pair a sponsor placement with a practical demonstration of your AI tool or service robot.

Example: a showroom demo +

At an approved venue, demonstrate an AI assistant turning an inquiry into a booked consultation, or a robot performing an agreed task. Film the actual outcome and invite visitors to a follow-up demo.

Make it worth sharing.

Turn a placement into a coordinated set of photographs, short films and a measurable campaign.

A technology or lifestyle partner sponsors the rear wing for an agreed content series. Define the shots, usage rights and campaign dates in advance. Report published views and attributable visits after release.
