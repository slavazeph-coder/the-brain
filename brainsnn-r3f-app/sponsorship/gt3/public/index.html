BUYER FAQ

The details that matter.

What exactly am I requesting? +

Advertising on one selected panel of the proposed GT3 campaign. A door means one side, not both. This is not a share of a vehicle or an investment. During prelaunch, your artwork and email form a private, non-binding inquiry.

Does the price include the wrap, events and content? +

The accepted campaign offer determines what is included. Before buying, check installation and removal, agreed appearances, photo or video deliverables, usage rights, reporting and any extra charges. The use-case examples are ideas, not an included package.

How big will my ad be? +

The model shows the proposed location and a visual preview, not measured print dimensions. The final printable area and artwork layout must be approved separately. The size control changes your preview, not the purchased panel or price.

Can I try my artwork without paying or publishing it? +

Yes. You can upload artwork, compare panels and rotate the car privately in your browser. It is only sent to BrainSNN when you submit with permission. It is not automatically published as a confirmed sponsor.
