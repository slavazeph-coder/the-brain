Is the car already secured?

This is a prelaunch sponsorship concept. Vehicle access, insurance, campaign dates, artwork rights and sufficient funding must be confirmed before any commitment to delivery.

What do the prices mean?

They are provisional floors derived from the campaign funding model, not live bids or guaranteed packages. You can propose a budget; it does not reserve a placement. Final scope and price require a written agreement.

When can I pay?

No public checkout is active for this car campaign. A proposal is not a payment, invoice or investment. Payment arrangements follow a separately agreed campaign, fulfillment and tax review.

What happens to my information?

Proposal details are stored privately for review and scheduled for deletion after 180 days. No uploaded logo is attached to the proposal. Artwork remains in this browser unless you separately permit rendering in Sketchfab. Share links contain only your selected placement and mode. Contact info@xioai.ca about access or deletion.

Why might 3D take longer to load?

The original model is hosted by Sketchfab and depends on browser graphics support and that service. Standard viewer opens it without our custom tools. Camera or logo-tool errors do not remove a loaded car. Studio images are labelled illustrations, not proof of a live 3D render.
