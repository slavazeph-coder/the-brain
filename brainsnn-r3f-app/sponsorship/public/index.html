A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.

Something else on your mind?
Talk to XIO ↗

Am I buying the robot?

No. You are applying for advertising or campaign services associated with Robot 001. The robot remains with its operator. Hardware ownership and acquisition are not established by this page.

Is this a live auction?

Not yet. You can submit a private offer or a fixed-price request for review. There are no invented bidders, public winning bids or countdowns. A formal auction would require published rules and a confirmed schedule before opening.

What does the 90-day term include?

The proposed campaign term starts on the first activation date in the signed agreement. Actual venue dates, appearance hours, installation, content quantities, usage rights, category conflicts and measurement are agreed before payment. A 90-day term does not mean continuous operation.

Can I see my actual logo on the robot?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG or WebP and choose a placement in the studio. Your logo is rendered on the G1 reference geometry in your browser. The preview is illustrative; safe dimensions and final placement need operator approval. Logos cannot obscure cameras, cooling, joints or safety markings.

What happens when I submit?

Your application is saved privately and you receive a reference on screen. It is not a reservation, a winning bid or a charge. XIO reviews the request before agreeing a campaign. An email acknowledgment is not automatically sent.

Where will the robot appear?

Potential formats include approved retail events, conferences, launches and controlled content shoots. Venue permission, staffing, insurance and the schedule must be confirmed. No named mall, sponsor or event is represented as a partner.

What about livestreams and audience data?

Livestreaming is a future option requiring a separate production and privacy plan. It is not promised in these packages. We do not use facial recognition in this website or preview. Campaign measurement must be agreed and should prioritize aggregate activity and consented inquiries.

When do I pay, and what happens if plans change?

Only after an approved scope and written agreement should you receive a private payment invitation. Payment availability depends on the merchant configuration. Cancellation, refunds, rescheduling and any launch conditions must be written into that agreement before payment; this application is free.
